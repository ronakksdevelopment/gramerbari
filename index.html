About the Resort

A village home, built for modern comfort

Tucked beside a quiet lake and shaded by old mango and palm trees, Gramer Bari Resort was built to give city dwellers a genuine taste of rural Bengal, thatched roofs, courtyards, and the kind of silence you only find far from traffic. Every cottage is handcrafted using local materials, paired with modern plumbing, comfortable bedding and reliable power backup.

Stay & Dine

Everything a village retreat should offer

From mud cottages to riverside feasts, each experience at Gramer Bari is designed around comfort, nature and real local flavour.

01

Thatched Cottages

Mud-finish cottages with private verandahs, modern bathrooms and courtyard seating facing the lake.

02

Farm-to-Table Dining

Traditional Bengali thalis cooked on wood-fire stoves using vegetables from our own organic farm.

03

Boating & Fishing

Paddle boats on the private pond and guided fishing sessions for guests of all ages.

04

Bonfire & Folk Music

Evening bonfires with live baul and folk performances under the open sky, every weekend.

05

Village Cycle Tours

Guided rides through paddy fields, local markets and nearby heritage temples.

06

Events & Day Outings

Custom packages for birthdays, corporate offsites, school picnics and family reunions.
